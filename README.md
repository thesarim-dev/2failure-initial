## 1. Introduction

**2failure** is a minimal and mobile-friendly workout tracker built for daily strength training with minimal equipment. You pick exercises, complete timed or rep-tracked sets, log your performance, and earn coins to unlock harder variations.

The app is organized around a simple daily loop:

```
Sign in → Pick an exercise → Complete the set → Log reps/weight → Earn coins → Repeat
```

This guide walks through every screen and action in that loop, step by step.

## 2. Before you begin

### What you need

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Device** | Smartphone, tablet, or computer with a modern web browser |
| **Internet** | Required to sign in and sync progress |
| **Camera** | Optional — used only for AI rep tracking on supported exercises |
| **Equipment** | None required to start. Floor space covers the starter moves. A bench or stairs, a pull-up bar and dip bars (found in most public parks), and a backpack you can load with books or water bottles unlock the rest. No gym needed. |

### What 2failure tracks

- Daily set completion per exercise
- Workout streaks (consecutive training days)
- Rep personal bests for bodyweight moves
- Weight × rep personal bests for loaded exercises
- Coins earned per completed set

## 4. Your first session (the onboarding quest)

New players are guided through one short, hands-on quest the first time they sign in:

1. **Welcome:** the whole idea in three steps: *train to failure → earn materials → build your base*.
2. **Your base:** the app opens the Base tab and introduces your plot.
3. **Build your first building, free:** tap **Build**, pick the **Lodge** (marked **FREE**), choose a spot and tap a glowing tile. Construction starts: the Lodge needs **one set** to finish.
4. **Build it with a workout:** tap **Train**, then the first exercise (Pushups). Do as many good reps as you can, until you can't do one more. That's training to failure. A coach guides you through the set.
5. **Your set finishes the Lodge:** the receipt shows *Lodge is finished!*; tap **See your base**.
6. **Celebrate:** a quick recap of the basics: 2 sets a day keep your flame lit, **Next up** shows what to build next, coins unlock bigger buildings and land, and **Edit plan** sets your days and equipment.

A coach bubble points at what to tap at every step and moves on when you do it. **Skip** ends the quest at any time. To see the classic tour of the home screen again, use **Settings → Replay tour**.

## 5. Understand the home screen

The home screen is your command center. Everything you need for today's session is here.

### Header bar

| Element | Location | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **2failure logo** | Top left | Branding — not tappable |
| **Settings** (gear icon) | Top right | Opens preferences and FAQ |
| **Coins badge** | Top right | Shows your current coin balance |

### Streak badge

The flame shows your **current streak**. It lights up once today is protected (your second set, or a rest day). Your streak counts **days on plan**: days you train (at least two sets, any exercises) plus rest days. You can rest up to a set number of days in any rolling 7-day window: **7 minus your weekly training target** (4 rest days at 3 days a week, 3 at 4 days, 2 at 5 days). Missed days count as rest automatically, so you don't have to tap **Rest day** (that button just swaps today to stretches). The streak only breaks when you take more rest days in a 7-day window than your allowance, or after about two weeks off. When you train again after resting, the rest days are added to your streak count.

If your streak breaks (more rest than your allowance), a **Restore streak** banner appears on the home screen. It does not appear just because you have not trained yet today, or because your current streak is below your longest. Restore is unlimited. The first restore each month costs **100 coins**, then the price doubles with each use (**200**, **400**, **800**) and caps at **1,600**. The price resets to 100 at the start of each month.

### Fun fact panel

### Exercise cards

Below the header, **PICK YOUR WORKOUT** lists today's equipped exercises. Each card shows:

- **Exercise name** — e.g., Pushups, Squats, Planks
- **Set progress** — `completed / goal` sets for the day (e.g., `1 / 3 sets`)
- **Description** — a one-line cue for how to perform the move
- **Equipment label** — shown when the exercise needs gear

Tap a card to start that exercise. Cards marked **done** are dimmed once you've finished their sets for today.

### Today's day

Above the cards: which day of your week it is and what it trains, for example **Day 1 / 3 · Push day · Chest, shoulders, triceps**.

## 6. Complete a workout

When you tap an exercise card, the workout screen opens. What you see depends on the exercise type.

### Timed workouts (most exercises)

Used for holds (planks, L-sit), stretches, and exercises without AI tracking.

1. The timer starts when the workout screen loads.
2. Perform the exercise at your own pace.
3. When you finish the set, tap the **finish** button.
4. Proceed to [log reps and weight](#7-log-reps-and-weight) or the [set summary](#8-review-your-set-summary).

### AI rep tracking (supported bodyweight moves)

Available for exercises like pushups, squats, and lunges when AI tracking is enabled.

1. Allow camera access when prompted.
2. Position yourself in frame. The status indicator shows:
   - **get in position** — follow the instructions for the starting position
   - **hold still** — maintain the position
   - **starting...** — a countdown from 3 to 1
   - **tracking reps** — reps are being counted
   - **camera off** / **camera paused** — tracking is unavailable or paused
3. Perform your set. The rep counter updates in real time.
4. Tap the **finish** button when done. Tracked reps are carried forward to the logging step.
5. Toggle **front cam** / **back cam** if you need to switch cameras.
6. Tap **pause cam** / **resume cam** to pause tracking without ending the set.
7. Switch to **Use timer only** if you prefer not to use the camera.

### Cancel a workout

Tap **Cancel workout** to return to the home screen without logging a set. No coins are earned and set progress is not incremented.

### Streak

With no streak yet, the flame's sign reads "Start today" instead of a grey zero.

### Accessibility

Tile labels are 12px or larger, the rest banner's buttons are 44px touch targets, and the light-mode tally marks and "Start today" sign have proper contrast.

### Today's progress and beat last time

* **Today at a glance:** under the day heading, a progress bar ("4 of 11 sets today · about 18 min left") above the exercise list.
* **A target for every set:** the workout screen shows your last result for that exercise: "Last time: 14 reps · beat it!" (or "Last set: 14 reps" when it was earlier today, since beating a set to failure straight away isn't realistic).
* **Faster logging:** the rep box opens prefilled with that number (selected, so typing replaces it), with big −/+ buttons.

### Home header

* **Resources:** one HUD strip (stone, timber, crystal and coins) using the same icons as the Base screen.
* **Rest day ticket:** "Rest day", tally marks with how many are left, and "Slide to tear". Drag the zipper along the perforation (about 60% of the way is enough); the bottom half falls off and the top half stays, centred on the row: "Rest day · Streak safe today". A tap nudges the zipper; keyboards can press Enter on it.
* **Night falls on a rest day:** a dusk wash sweeps down, the home screen turns to an indigo night (lavender in light mode), stars come out and a crescent moon rises, while the training list hands over to the rest-day stretches. Opening the app on a rest day just shows the night sky.
* **Your plan as a departure board:** the plan's days a week on a split-flap digit ("5 · days a week · Tap to change"). Tapping clacks the flap over and opens your plan; change the days and the board flips flap by flap to the new number when you come back.

### Rest timer

After every set a banner drops down from the top of the screen and stays while you move around the app (the page shifts down so it never covers anything). Its colour slides smoothly from green through yellow to red as the rest runs out; at zero it chimes, buzzes and offers **Start set**. Adjust with −15 s / +15 s, or skip with ✕. Rest length follows the evidence (`src/lib/restTimes.ts`): barbell lifts 3:00, hard and loaded moves 2:00, standard bodyweight 1:30, core / calves / holds 1:00, moving on to the next exercise 1:00, and no timer after the day's last set.

### Progress (in Settings)

Open it from **Settings → Your progress → View**. Sets this week, streak, all sets in the last 6 months and new records this month; a 12-week training calendar (rest days in violet); sets per week split into upper body / legs / core; every exercise with its best set, improvement since the first session and a trend chart; and a list of personal records.

### Coins are server-authoritative (anti-cheat)

Coins are earned and spent on the server (`supabase/migrations/20261026120000_server_coin_rewards.sql`). The app reports a finished set (exercise, duration, tier) to `log_set_reward`, which checks it's plausible (a set can't end sooner after the previous one than it lasted; at most 80 rewarded sets a day; durations are clamped) and pays with the same formula as `src/lib/coinRewards.ts`. Spending goes through `spend_coins`, which checks the balance. Every change is recorded in a `coin_ledger` table, and apps can no longer write the coins column directly. Until the migration is applied the app falls back to the old behaviour, so nothing breaks in between.

**The base economy is enforced too** (`supabase/migrations/20261027120000_server_economy.sql`):

* **Materials and buildings:** every player has an earned material budget, raised by each server-checked set (+25 per set, +60 on the first set of a day for quests and the session bonus, +40 on the first set of a week for the weekly bonus). Whenever the base is saved, the server totals its value: the materials it holds plus everything built from them, priced from the game's own catalog (`src/game/economy.ts`, exported into the SQL by `scripts/economy-sql.ts`). A base worth more than the budget is refused, and the app restores the real saved base. Existing players start with a budget covering their current base, so nobody loses anything.
* **The set log** (behind Progress and future leaderboards) is written only through `log_set_entry`, which rejects implausible numbers (over 1,000 reps or 500 kg), entries faster than one every 3 seconds, and more than 300 a day.
* **Streaks** can only grow by one day with real training in the last 48 hours, restores can't exceed the longest streak ever reached, and total workouts count up one at a time.

### Today's progress follows the plan slot

Each training day is a set of slots (chest, shoulders, triceps…), each with a ladder of exercises from easiest to hardest. Progress belongs to the **slot**, not the exercise: do 1 set of pushups, go to **Edit plan** and tap **Harder**, and the chest slot shows **Backpack Pushups 1 / 3** (not 0 / 3), and your next set is "Set 2 of 3".

How it's counted (`slotProgress` in `src/lib/plan.ts`): sets are still stored per exercise in Supabase (no schema change). A set counts toward a slot if it was logged for that slot (remembered on the device), or it's the slot's current exercise, or it's anywhere on that slot's ladder. Each set counts once and never beyond the slot's target.

The receipt after a set says **Set logged!** (or **Set 2 of 3 logged**); the receipt for the **last set of today's whole plan** says **Workout done!**

### The exercise catalog

59 exercises (54 for training days plus 5 rest-day stretches), organised into the plan's slots. Every slot is a ladder that runs easiest → hardest (BASE → PRO → ELITE), so **Harder** always means harder.

| Day | Slots |
|---|---|
| Push | chest · shoulders · triceps · abs · core |
| Pull | back · mid back · upper back · abs · core |
| Legs | quads · single leg · **hamstrings (hip hinge)** · calves · obliques |
| Full body | push · pull · legs · glutes · core |

Highlights: a real **hip hinge** (single-leg RDLs → backpack/dumbbell RDLs → barbell deadlifts → nordic curls), a full **shoulder** ladder (incline pike → pike → dumbbell/barbell press → elevated pike → wall handstand pushups), **anti-rotation core** work (dead bugs, bird dogs), and gear that matters: dumbbells unlock 4 exercises, a barbell 5, a pull-up bar 7.

### AI camera tracking (every exercise)

Every exercise can use the camera. MediaPipe's pose model runs **on your phone** (frames never leave the device) and reads 33 body points.

* **Rep exercises** watch one measurement and count a rep on each full cycle. Half reps don't count, and a short wobble or a moment out of view can't fake or lose a rep.

  | Family | What's measured | Exercises |
  |---|---|---|
  | Push | elbow angle | all pushup and pike variants, handstand pushups, dips, bench press, shoulder presses (archer pushups watch the bending arm) |
  | Pull | elbow angle | pull-ups, chin-ups, negatives, backpack pull-ups, all rows (bodyweight, dumbbell, barbell), superman pulls |
  | Arms lift | hand height | prone Y-T-W raises |
  | Squat | knee angle | squats, jump/backpack/goblet/barbell squats; lunges, split squats, pistols and step-ups watch the working leg |
  | Hinge | torso angle | single-leg, backpack and dumbbell RDLs, barbell deadlifts; nordic curls watch the knee open |
  | Hip | hip angle | glute and single-leg bridges, leg raises, hanging knee and leg raises, dead bugs, bird dogs, side plank hip dips; crunches watch the torso curl |
  | Feet | heel lift | calf raises, single-leg calf raises |

* **Holds** (planks, side planks, knee side planks, hollow body, L-sit and the stretches) get a **form check** instead of a rep count: only the seconds you spend in good form count, and a live chip says *Good form: hold it!* or what to fix (*Keep your hips level*, *Lift your legs*…).
* Before a set starts, the camera waits until you're in the starting position (top of a pushup, standing, hanging, lying for bridges…) and tells you if you're not.
* Each exercise shows a camera-placement hint for its view (side-on at floor level, side-on standing, facing the bar, or seated).
* Camera-counted reps are logged as **verified** (they count toward verified trophies). Weighted moves open the weight prompt with the camera's reps filled in. **Use timer only** is always available.

AI tracking is on by default; set `VITE_ENABLE_POSE_AI=false` to turn it off. Developers: `npm run test:pose` runs a synthetic test of every exercise (full reps, half reps, jitter, dropouts, starting positions and holds), and `npm run test:plan` checks the plan (every ladder goes easiest → hardest, no dead ends, every exercise reachable).

## 7. Log reps and weight

After you tap finish, 2failure may ask you to confirm what you accomplished. The prompt depends on the exercise.

### Bodyweight rep logging

For exercises like pushups, squats, lunges, and pull-ups:

1. Enter the number of reps you completed.
2. If AI tracking was used, the count may be pre-filled — adjust it if needed.
3. Tap **log it**.

You must enter at least **1 rep** to continue.

### Weighted exercise logging

For backpack exercises (backpack pushups, squats and pull-ups), log how heavy your loaded backpack was:

1. Review the set label (e.g., **Set 2 of 3**) and the intensity cue:
   - **Leave 1–2 reps in the tank** — stop before failure (RIR 1–2)
   - **Leave 1 rep in the tank** — stop one rep short of failure
   - **Go to absolute failure** — final set; push to your limit
2. Enter the **backpack weight** using the stepper buttons (2.5 kg or 5 lb steps). A bathroom scale works: weigh yourself with and without the backpack.
3. Enter **reps**.
4. Reference lines show your **last session** load and **today's sets so far**.
5. Tap **log set**.

### After logging

- If more sets remain for this exercise, the summary screen offers **next set** to jump back into the workout.
- When all sets are complete, tap **back home** to return to the dashboard.

## 8. Review your set summary

The summary screen confirms what you logged and celebrates milestones.

### What you see

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Set progress** | e.g., "Set 2 of 3 logged" when mid-exercise |
| **Duration** | How long the timed portion lasted |
| **Coins earned** | How many coins you received this set |
| **Reps / load × reps** | What you logged this set |
| **Personal best** | Your all-time best for this exercise |
| **Progression tip** | Suggested weight adjustment for next session (backpack moves) |
| **Time to level up** | Shown when a bodyweight set goes past the move's useful range (see below) |

If your set ran longer than 2 minutes, the receipt also reminds you that coin rewards stop at 2 minutes and recommends trying a harder exercise in the Store or adding weight. This note is skipped when the level-up card already appears.

### Personal bests and progression

- A **new rep personal best** or **new weight PR** badge appears when you beat a previous record.
- For weighted exercises, 2failure may suggest increasing, maintaining, or decreasing load based on your performance — including fatigue detection when your last set drops significantly compared to your first.

### Level up when a move gets easy

Doing 50 pushups in a set means pushups have become endurance work. Strength and muscle grow faster when a set ends near failure in fewer, harder reps. Every bodyweight move has a rep ceiling (holds have a time ceiling). When you pass it, the receipt shows **Time to level up** and names the next harder moves to try.

| Example move | Level up after | Try next |
|---|---|---|
| Pushups | 20 reps | Diamond pushups, backpack pushups |
| Squats | 25 reps | Bulgarian splits, backpack squats |
| Pull-ups | 12 reps | Backpack pull-ups |
| Planks | 90 seconds | Hollow body hold, side planks |

At the top of a ladder, the receipt suggests slowing each rep down (3 seconds lowering, pause at the bottom) or wearing a loaded backpack instead. Backpack moves progress by adding weight, using the progression tip.

### Celebration

When you finish all sets for an exercise, confetti appears on the summary screen. Tap **back home** to continue your session or end for the day.

## 9. Your plan

There's one way to train: **your plan**. You answer two questions and the app tells you what to do each day.

Open it from **Edit workout** on the home screen (or **Settings → Your plan**).

**Days per week: 3, 4 or 5.** The app rotates day types for you:

| Days | Your week |
|---|---|
| 3 | Push · Legs · Pull |
| 4 | Push · Legs · Pull · Full body |
| 5 | Push · Legs · Pull · Legs · Full body |

**Pick today's day:** in **Your week**, tap any day to train it today (for example, do Pull day instead of Legs day). The rotation carries on from there. Today's exercises are listed right below.

**Your equipment:** tap the gym equipment at your disposal: **pull-up bar**, **dip bars**, **dumbbells** or **barbell**. Each card shows what it unlocks (for example, a pull-up bar unlocks pull-ups and chin-ups). Exercises that only need a chair, step or backpack are always included. With nothing selected, every day still works.

## 10. Today's exercises

Each day has five exercises, one per muscle job, balanced so push and pull get equal work:

| Day | Muscles | Example (no equipment) | Example (full gym) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Push | Chest, shoulders, triceps, abs, core | Pushups, pike pushups, diamond pushups, leg raises, planks | Bench press, pike pushups, diamond pushups, leg raises, planks |
| Pull | Back, mid back, upper back, abs, core | Doorway rows, floor rows, superman pulls, crunches, hollow body | Pull-ups, inverted rows, superman pulls, crunches, hollow body |
| Legs | Quads, single leg, hamstrings, calves, obliques | Squats, lunges, single-leg bridges, calf raises, side planks | Goblet squats, lunges, single-leg bridges, calf raises, side planks |
| Full body | Push, pull, legs, glutes, core | Pushups, doorway rows, jump squats, glute bridges, planks | Dips, doorway rows, jump squats, glute bridges, planks |

The first exercise of each day is the main one (3 sets); the rest are 2 sets.

**Easier / Harder.** In **Your plan**, each of today's exercises has **Easier** and **Harder** buttons that move along its ladder (for example: pushups → archer pushups → backpack pushups → dips → bench press). Only exercises your equipment allows are offered.

**Leveling up.** When a set shows you've outgrown an exercise, the receipt offers **Switch to …** the next harder one. One tap, and it's in your plan from next time.

**All exercises are free.** There's no exercise shop: coins are for your base (buildings, land, decorations) and streak restores.

**Rest days:** tap **Rest day** on the home screen; today becomes stretches and your streak stays safe (see the streak rule in section 12).

## 12. Track progress over time

### Coins

You earn coins after every finished set of **10 seconds or more**:

> **Coins = 20 + effort bonus (up to 10, full at 60 seconds) + tier bonus (Pro +5, Elite +10)**

| Set | Coins |
|---|---:|
| Under 10 seconds | 0 |
| 30-second Base set | 25 |
| 35-second Base set | 26 |
| 45-second Pro set | 33 |
| 60+ second Elite set | 40 (the maximum) |

A typical set earns about **25–30 coins**. Spend them on new exercises, base upgrades, land and streak restores.
- A 1-minute Pro exercise earns 10 coins.
- A 2-minute Elite exercise earns 30 coins.
- Any set longer than 2 minutes still earns the same maximum coins for that tier.

If a set goes over 2 minutes, the receipt recommends switching to a harder exercise in the Store or making the current move harder by adding weight. Spend coins in the store to unlock exercise variations. Your balance appears in the header on the home screen, store, and settings.

### Streaks

Your streak counts **days on plan**: days you train (at least two sets, any exercises) plus rest days. You can rest up to a set number of days in any rolling 7-day window: **7 minus your weekly training target** (4 rest days at 3 days a week, 3 at 4 days, 2 at 5 days). Missed days count as rest automatically, so you don't have to tap **Rest day** (that button just swaps today to stretches). The streak only breaks when you take more rest days in a 7-day window than your allowance, or after about two weeks off. When you train again after resting, the rest days are added to your streak count. A broken streak restarts at 1 on your next training day unless you restore it.

If your streak breaks, a **Restore streak** banner appears on the home screen until you log two sets that day (a Lodge shield restores it for free). You can restore as many times as you want. Cost starts at **100 coins** each month and doubles after every restore until it reaches **1,600**, then stays there until the next month.

### Pushup daily goal

On push days, the pushups card shows progress toward a daily rep target (e.g., `45 / 100 pushups`) across all sets.

### Personal bests

Rep and weight personal bests are recorded automatically when you log sets. The summary screen highlights new records. Weighted exercises track your best **load × reps** combination.

## 13. Troubleshooting

### I cannot sign in

| Symptom | What to try |
|---|---|
| "Could not sign in" | Verify email and password. Use **Sign Up** if you have no account. |
| Google sign-in fails | Ensure pop-ups are allowed. Check that you selected the correct Google account. |
| Session does not restore | Sign out and sign in again. Check your internet connection. |

### Progress did not save

- Confirm you are signed in (progress is account-based).
- Complete the full flow: finish the set → log reps/weight → return home.
- If you see **Could not save this set**, check your connection and tap **log set** again.

### Camera / AI tracking issues

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **camera off** | Permission denied or no camera found | Grant camera access in browser settings and reload. |
| **camera paused** | Tracking paused manually | Tap **resume cam**. |
| **loading AI...** | Model is loading | Wait a few seconds. |
| **get in position** | Not in starting pose | Follow on-screen guidance for lighting, framing, and pose. |
| Tracking counts incorrectly | Pose not fully visible | Adjust angle, lighting, or distance. Use **timer only** as a fallback. |

### Your plan

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| An exercise is too easy or too hard | Open **Your plan** and tap **Easier** or **Harder** next to it. |
| An exercise needs equipment I don't have | Open **Your plan** and untick that equipment; the plan switches to something you can do. |

### Data loading errors

If the home screen shows a red error banner:

- **Could not load set progress** → refresh the page.
- **Could not load workout stats** → check connection; streak may show a spinner until resolved.
- **Could not refresh profile** → sign out and back in.

## Your base (the game)

### Sound

All sounds are synthesized live (no audio files), through a small sound engine (`src/lib/feedback.ts`): a soft room reverb, a gentle compressor and a safety limiter, with slight pitch and stereo variation on every play so repeats never feel robotic. The voices are themed to the village: wooden knocks for taps, bells for rewards, a hammer thud when placing, a sparkle chime when a build finishes (it plays when you next open the Base), a little fanfare for levelling up, and a soft wooden "bonk" (never a harsh beep) for errors.

**Ambient sounds** (`src/lib/ambient.ts`): while the Base is open you hear a quiet soundscape: a breeze and birdsong, plus babbling water if your base has water and crackling if it has a campfire, torch or forge. It sits well below the effects, fades in and out, pauses when the app is in the background, and can be switched off in **Settings → Ambient sounds** (it also follows the main **Sounds** switch).

### Life on the base

Decorations bring the base to life:

* **Training equipment:** by day, a villager trains on each piece: pull-ups on the bar, dips on the dip bars, punches on the bag, box jumps on the plyo box.
* **Campfire:** at night (7 pm to 6 am, local time) villagers gather on logs around it, in its warm glow; the equipment stands empty.
* **Beehive:** bees buzz around it (like butterflies over flowers and garden beds).
* **Lily pads:** ducks come and paddle around them.
* **Coop and barn:** more chickens, cows, sheep and pigs.
* **Paths:** villagers stroll along the paths you paint, there and back, instead of cutting across the grass.

### Seasonal decorations

**Pumpkins** can only be built in autumn (September to November) and the **snowman** in winter (December to February), open to every base level while in season. The build menu shows the days left ("Autumn only · 36 days left"), or when an item is back ("Back in winter"). Once placed, seasonal items stay all year as keepsakes.

### Decorations

Buildings and most decorations are drawn in **true isometric** with one shared light (lit left face, shaded right face) and shared materials (stone, wood, thatch, metal, marble), so everything on the map looks like it belongs to the same world. Decorations drawn this way: wall, garden bed, flower arch, flowers, banner, windmill, bridge, lily pads, pull-up bar, dip bars, punching bag, lanterns, parasol, signpost, statue (Flex and Runner) and well. Their colour and style options all still apply.

### Paths, fences and walls

**Placing buildings: preview, then confirm.** After choosing something to build (or tapping Move on a building), tap any highlighted tile: a see-through preview of the building appears there on a lime footprint, and nothing is built or paid yet. Tap other tiles to try different spots, then tap **Place here** (or **Move here**) to confirm, or **Cancel**.

**Painting the ground: three tools.** In **Paint**, pick a ground and a tool:
* **Box** (default): tap one corner, then the opposite corner, and everything between fills. A live preview shows exactly what will be painted, and two corners on the same row make a straight line (handy for paths). Dragging still pans the map.
* **Fill**: tap a patch of ground to repaint the whole connected patch of that same ground at once.
* **Brush**: drag across tiles to paint them one by one.

**Paths are painted like terrain.** Choose **Path** in the build menu (or pick it in **Paint**) and drag across the ground to lay cobblestones. Paths no longer take up a square of their own, and older path pieces are converted automatically.

**Fences, walls and hedges run between tiles.** Choose **Wall** in the build menu to open the fence tool and pick **Wooden fence** (2 timber per piece), **Stone wall** (3 stone) or **Hedge** (1 timber, 1 crystal). Then **drag along the lines between tiles** to draw: a glowing line shows exactly where the next piece will go, and you can turn corners in the same stroke. Start a stroke on an existing piece to **erase** instead. A single tap adds or removes one piece. Pieces join seamlessly: walls flow as one continuous run with piers only where they turn or end, and fence pieces share their posts.

### Every building and decoration has its own story

Buildings carry the apparatus of their job: the Watchtower has a ladder and an alarm bell, the Forge an anvil and hammer, a quench barrel and a coal heap, the Training Yard a straw dummy and a stand of weight plates, the Crystal Spring reeds and a frog on a stone, the Garden a watering can, and stone walls are climbed by ivy. Decorations look used: a book and a mug on the bench, a pot over the campfire, a pigeon on the statue, a towel on the pull-up bar, gloves by the punching bag, a bucket on the well, a lantern and boots by the tent, a basket on the picnic table, a jack-o'-lantern among the pumpkins, a bluebird at the birdhouse, a butterfly over the flowers, a frog on the lily pad, and more.

### Materials, light and sky

Buildings have real materials: stone walls show cut-stone courses and wooden walls show planks, both following the isometric perspective, and roofs have rows of scalloped shingles. Everything that stands up casts a soft shadow away from the light, walls darken slightly where they meet the ground, cloud shadows drift slowly over the land, and the grass has gentle colour patches. The island floats in a sky (blue by day in light mode, a starry dusk in dark mode) with clouds drifting behind it.

### A lived-in base

Your base feels inhabited: **villagers** stroll between your buildings (one for the HQ, more as your Lodge grows; some carry baskets or firewood) and stop to look around. Windows always glow warmly (brighter on days you train, and a few flicker like candlelight), chimneys smoke, flags flutter, sparks rise from the Forge, and buildings get lived-in details: firewood and a chopping block by the Lodge, flower boxes under its windows, barrels and crates by the HQ and a lantern at its door.

### Performance (how the map stays smooth)

The ground (terrain, grass, flowers, edges and the cut-away sides) is about 16,000 drawing elements, so the map **bakes it into a single image** and draws only the living parts live: buildings, decorations, trophies, animals and fireflies. The ground is re-baked automatically when it changes (painting, expanding land, placing or moving something); while you paint, the ground is drawn live so changes appear instantly. On a fully built base this cut the map from about 18,600 elements to about 2,100, and from 1,194 simultaneous animations to 95.

### The Base tab at a glance

- **The map comes first.** Floating on it: your **base level** (top left, with a ring showing progress to the next level), your **builders** (top right; tap to see what's being built), and the **Build** button with **Expand land** and **Paint** beside it.
- **Next up** sits under the map: your next goals, worked out from the game's rules and in the order that makes sense (for example, a **Lodge** comes before the **HQ upgrade** that needs its second builder). The top goal shows a checklist of exactly what's still missing, with about how many sets that is (for example *timber 14/22 · ≈ 2 leg sets*, *coins 120/300 · ≈ 7 sets*). When a goal is ready, one tap starts it.
- **Today** (plan progress, weekly bonus and quests) is below that.

Every set you finish earns materials for your own base. Tap **Base** in the bottom bar to see it.

**Coins:** every finished set (10 seconds or more) earns 20 coins, plus up to 10 for a longer set and 5 or 10 more for pro or elite moves, so a typical set earns about 25–30.

**Materials** come from the kind of set you do: upper-body sets (push and pull) earn stone, lower-body sets earn timber, core sets earn crystal, and stretches earn 1 of each. A typical set earns about 3; bigger sets earn more, scaled to how hard the exercise is (10 pushups ≈ 3, 30 ≈ 9; 12 pull-ups ≈ 6), and longer holds or a heavier backpack earn more too, up to 12 per set. Coins are separate and still pay for land, upgrades and the store.

**Today's plan** is exactly what the Train tab shows: today's day from your plan. Sets in today's plan earn full materials. A few sets beyond the plan earn half, then nothing, because rest is part of training. Finishing the whole plan earns a session bonus. Sets under 10 seconds don't earn anything.

**Your week**: hitting your weekly training target (your days per week in **Your plan**) earns the biggest reward of the week and builds a weekly streak.

**Streaks respect rest.** Days off within your weekly rest allowance (7 minus your training days) keep your streak alive and count as days on plan. Only going beyond the allowance breaks it.

| Building | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Headquarters** | The heart of the base. Other buildings can't be a higher level than HQ. Level 2 is open from the start (build a Lodge first for the second builder); level 3 also needs 2 weeks on target. |
| **Watchtower** | Daily quests from today's plan. Each level adds a quest slot. |
| **Training Yard** | Shows today's workout as stations; raises the session bonus. |
| **Lodge** | +1 builder per level (one Lodge per base), and stores streak shields for unplanned misses. |
| **Forge** | Trades one material for another (3:1, or 2:1 at level 3). |
| **Crystal Spring** | Bonus for stretches and for your first set after a rest day. |

**Building and upgrading.** Open **Build** and pick from three tabs: **Buildings**, **Decor** and **Trophies**. Every job needs builders while it's in progress: new buildings, decorations and trophies take 1 builder; upgrading to level 2 takes 2 builders at once, and level 3 takes 3. You start with 1 builder, and each Lodge level adds one. Jobs finish by completing sets, not by waiting, and several can run at once when you have free builders.

Buildings go up to level 3, and each level looks different: rustic, then reinforced, then modern. Upgrades cost materials plus coins (150 for level 2 and 400 for level 3; Headquarters costs 250 and 600).

**Customize.** Before placing something, pick its style and colour: trim colours for buildings, styles for trees, flower beds, lamps, flags, paths, walls and fountains, and pedestals for trophies. Tap anything you've placed and choose **Customize** to change it again at any time, for free.

**Base level.** Everything you build, upgrade and earn adds points to your base level, which moves through titles from Campsite to Legend (Campsite, Outpost, Hamlet, Village, Town, Stronghold, Citadel, Capital, Legend). Each level unlocks new decorations and ground types; the card at the top of the Base tab always shows what the next level unlocks.

**Decorate freely.** There are 37 decorations in eight groups (Nature, Farm, Comfort, Fun, Lights, Training, Paths & walls, Water), including a calisthenics park (pull-up bar, dip bars, plyo box, punching bag) and a farm (barn, chicken coop, windmill, well, hay and more). Tap anything you've placed to customize, move or **Flip** it.

**Animals.** Cows, chickens, bunnies, cats, dogs and foxes wander your base, spawning in new spots and taking new routes each time you open it; bigger land brings more of them. Each **Barn** adds a cow, a sheep and a pig, each **Chicken coop** two chickens, and ponds get ducks. Animals really walk (legs swinging in step), stop to peck, graze or look around, and bunnies hop.  and butterflies flutter over flowers and meadows.

**Paint the ground.** Tap **Paint**, pick a ground (grass, dirt, meadow, sand, plaza, water, snow) and drag across tiles. Painting is free; more grounds unlock with base level. Water holds lily pads and bridges; everything else needs land.

**The map** is drawn in an isometric view: your land is a solid block of turf and earth floating on the page, and it grows as you expand it. A light grid appears only while you build, move or paint. **Pinch** to zoom on a phone (or scroll the mouse wheel), and drag to look around. Only the land itself responds to touch, so swiping beside it scrolls the page as usual.

**Land.** Your plot starts at 10×10. Tap **Expand land** to grow it with coins: 12×12 for 500, 14×14 for 1,200, 16×16 for 2,500, 18×18 for 4,000 and 20×20 for 6,000.

Decorations are refunded in full if removed. **Trophies** can only be earned, never bought; tap one to see its proof. Rep trophies counted by the AI camera are marked as verified.

The base is saved to your account in Supabase (table `base_games`), so it follows you across devices. A copy stays on the device so it opens instantly and works offline; it syncs again when you're back online.

## 14. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **RIR (Reps in Reserve)** | How many more reps you could have done before failure. "1–2 RIR" means you stopped with 1–2 reps left in the tank. |
| **Set** | One continuous bout of an exercise, from start to finish. |
| **Personal best (PR)** | Your highest recorded performance for an exercise — reps for bodyweight, load × reps for weighted moves. |
| **Your plan** | Days per week plus your equipment; the app rotates push, pull, legs and full-body days and picks each day's five exercises. |
| **Progressive overload** | Gradually increasing training stress (more weight, reps, or difficulty) to keep getting stronger. |
| **Tier** | Exercise difficulty rating in the store: base, pro, or elite. |
| **Ladder** | A sequence of versions of one movement, from easiest to hardest. Move up a rung when you pass the rep ceiling. |
| **Rep ceiling** | The top of a move's useful rep range. Past it, the receipt suggests leveling up. |
