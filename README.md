## 1. Introduction

**2failure** is a minimal and mobile-friendly workout tracker built for daily strength training with minimal equipment. You pick exercises, complete timed or rep-tracked sets, log your performance, and earn coins to unlock harder variations.

The app is organized around a simple daily loop:

```
Sign in → Pick an exercise → Complete the set → Log reps/weight → Earn coins → Repeat
```

This guide walks through every screen and action in that loop, step by step.

---

## 2. Before you begin

### What you need

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Device** | Smartphone, tablet, or computer with a modern web browser |
| **Internet** | Required to sign in and sync progress |
| **Camera** | Optional — used only for AI rep tracking on supported exercises |
| **Equipment** | None required to start. A chair, pull-up bar at a public park (or sturdy door frame), and floor space are enough for the base exercise library. Dumbbells and a barbell unlock additional moves. |

### What 2failure tracks

- Daily set completion per exercise
- Workout streaks (consecutive training days)
- Rep personal bests for bodyweight moves
- Weight × rep personal bests for loaded exercises
- Coins earned per completed set

## 3. Create an account and sign in

Your progress is tied to your account. Sign in before your first workout so sets, streaks, and coins sync across devices.

### Sign in with email

1. Open **2failure** in your browser.
2. On the login screen, enter your **email** and **password**.
3. Tap **Sign In**.

If you do not have an account yet:

1. Enter your email and choose a password.
2. Tap **Sign Up**.
3. Confirm your email if prompted, then sign in.

### Sign in with Google

1. On the login screen, tap **Sign in through Gmail**.
2. Choose your Google account when prompted.
3. You return to 2failure automatically once authentication completes.

### Switch appearance before signing in

The sun/moon icon on the login screen toggles between **day mode** and **night mode**. You can change this later in Settings.

## 5. Understand the home screen

The home screen is your command center. Everything you need for today's session is here.

### Header bar

| Element | Location | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **2failure logo** | Top left | Branding — not tappable |
| **Store** (bag icon) | Top right | Opens the exercise store |
| **Settings** (gear icon) | Top right | Opens preferences and FAQ |
| **Coins badge** | Top right | Shows your current coin balance |

### Streak badge

The orange flame badge shows your **current streak**: the number of consecutive days you have completed at least two sets (any exercises). The count updates after your second set of the day.

If a streak is at risk or already broken, a **Restore streak** action appears on the dashboard. Restore is always available at no cost and with no minimum streak length.

### Fun fact panel

A rotating panel of random general-information fun facts appears beside your streak badge. Facts refresh when you reload the app or change language.

### Exercise cards

Below the header, **PICK YOUR WORKOUT** lists today's equipped exercises. Each card shows:

- **Exercise name** — e.g., Pushups, Squats, Planks
- **Set progress** — `completed / goal` sets for the day (e.g., `1 / 3 sets`)
- **Description** — a one-line cue for how to perform the move
- **Equipment label** — shown in rotating-program mode when the exercise requires gear

Tap a card to start that exercise. Cards marked **done** are dimmed and cannot be tapped again until tomorrow (when using the rotating program) or until you have hit your daily set goal.

### Rotating program banner

When the rotating program is enabled, a line above the exercise list shows your current program day and focus, for example:

> Program day 3/5 — pull

## 6. Complete a workout

When you tap an exercise card, the workout screen opens. What you see depends on the exercise type.

### Timed workouts (most exercises)

Used for holds (planks, L-sit), stretches, and exercises without AI tracking.

1. The timer starts when the workout screen loads.
2. Perform the exercise at your own pace.
3. When you finish the set, tap the **finish** button.
4. Proceed to [log reps and weight](#7-log-reps-and-weight) or the [set summary](#8-review-your-set-summary).

### AI rep tracking (supported bodyweight moves)

Available for exercises like pushups, squats, and lunges when AI tracking is enabled.

1. Allow camera access when prompted.
2. Position yourself in frame. The status indicator shows:
   - **get in position** — follow the instructions for the starting position
   - **hold still** — maintain the position
   - **starting...** — a countdown from 3 to 1
   - **tracking reps** — reps are being counted
   - **camera off** / **camera paused** — tracking is unavailable or paused
3. Perform your set. The rep counter updates in real time.
4. Tap the **finish** button when done. Tracked reps are carried forward to the logging step.
5. Toggle **front cam** / **back cam** if you need to switch cameras.
6. Tap **pause cam** / **resume cam** to pause tracking without ending the set.
7. Switch to **Use timer only** if you prefer not to use the camera.

### Cancel a workout

Tap **Cancel workout** to return to the home screen without logging a set. No coins are earned and set progress is not incremented.

## 7. Log reps and weight

After you tap finish, 2failure may ask you to confirm what you accomplished. The prompt depends on the exercise.

### Bodyweight rep logging

For exercises like pushups, squats, lunges, and pull-ups:

1. Enter the number of reps you completed.
2. If AI tracking was used, the count may be pre-filled — adjust it if needed.
3. Tap **log it**.

You must enter at least **1 rep** to continue.

### Weighted exercise logging

For dumbbell, barbell, and bench exercises:

1. Review the set label (e.g., **Set 2 of 3**) and the intensity cue:
   - **Leave 1–2 reps in the tank** — stop before failure (RIR 1–2)
   - **Leave 1 rep in the tank** — stop one rep short of failure
   - **Go to absolute failure** — final set; push to your limit
2. Enter **weight** using the stepper buttons (increments match standard plate sizes: 2.5 kg or 5 lb).
3. Enter **reps**.
4. Reference lines show your **last session** load and **today's sets so far**.
5. Tap **log set**.

### After logging

- If more sets remain for this exercise, the summary screen offers **next set** to jump back into the workout.
- When all sets are complete, tap **back home** to return to the dashboard.

## 8. Review your set summary

The summary screen confirms what you logged and celebrates milestones.

### What you see

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **Set progress** | e.g., "Set 2 of 3 logged" when mid-exercise |
| **Duration** | How long the timed portion lasted |
| **Coins earned** | How many coins you received this set |
| **Reps / load × reps** | What you logged this set |
| **Personal best** | Your all-time best for this exercise |
| **Progression tip** | Suggested weight adjustment for next session (weighted moves) |

If your set ran longer than 2 minutes, the receipt also reminds you that coin rewards stop at 2 minutes and recommends trying a harder exercise in the Store or adding weight.

### Personal bests and progression

- A **new rep personal best** or **new weight PR** badge appears when you beat a previous record.
- For weighted exercises, 2failure may suggest increasing, maintaining, or decreasing load based on your performance — including fatigue detection when your last set drops significantly compared to your first.

### Celebration

When you finish all sets for an exercise, confetti appears on the summary screen. Tap **back home** to continue your session or end for the day.

## 9. Use the store and build your lineup

The store is where you unlock new exercises and choose which ones appear on your home screen.

### Open the store

Tap the **bag icon** in the home screen header.

### Buy an exercise

1. Browse sections: **Upperbody**, **Lowerbody**, and **Core**.
2. Locked exercises show a price in coins. Tap the price button to purchase.
3. You must have enough coins. Earn coins by completing sets (see [Track progress](#12-track-progress-over-time)).
4. Exercises priced at **0** are free and available immediately.

### Exercise tiers

Exercises in the Store are labeled by difficulty under the **equip** or **buy** button:

- **base** — starter moves (e.g. pushups, squats, planks)
- **pro** — intermediate (e.g. pull-ups, dips, jump squats)
- **elite** — advanced (e.g. barbell compounds, hollow body, Bulgarian splits)

Upper-body exercises also show **push** or **pull** beside the name. Unlock harder variations with coins as you progress.

### Equip exercises for your daily lineup

Each section lets you choose which unlocked exercises appear on the home screen:

| Section | Slots | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Upperbody** | 2 | Must include **1 push** and **1 pull** movement |
| **Lowerbody** | 2 | Any two lower-body exercises |
| **Core** | 2 | Any two core exercises |

**To equip:**

1. Expand a section (tap the header).
2. Tap **ACTIVE** on an equipped exercise to unequip.
3. Tap **EQUIP** on one of the highlighted exercises.

Equipped exercises show an **Active** badge.

### Rotating program lock

When the rotating program is on, lineup changes are **locked**. The program assigns exercises automatically. Turn the program off in Settings if you want manual control of your lineup again.

## 10. Follow the rotating program

The rotating program is an optional training plan built around **RIR** (reps in reserve), stopping just short of failure on early sets and going all-out on the final set. Pick the split that fits your week; rest (stretch) days are not fixed in the cycle — you choose them when you need them.

### Enable the program

1. Open **Settings** (gear icon).
2. Find **rotating program**.
3. Toggle it **On**.

### Training splits

Choose a split in Settings (under **training split**). Changing the split restarts the cycle from day 1.

| Split | Cycle |
|---|---|
| **3-day** | Push → Pull → Legs (classic PPL, highest frequency) |
| **4-day** | Push → Legs → Pull → Mixed (balanced, one combined day) |
| **5-day** | Push → Legs → Pull → Legs → Mixed (extra leg volume, default) |

The home screen banner shows **Program day X/N** and the current focus.

### Set prescriptions

| Exercise type | Sets per day | Intensity |
|---|---|---|
| **Equipment** (dumbbell, barbell, bench) | 3 | Sets 1–2: leave 1–2 reps in reserve. Set 3: go to failure. |
| **Bodyweight** | 2 | Set 1: leave 1 rep in reserve. Set 2: go to failure. |
| **Core** | 2 | Set 1: controlled pace. Set 2: max hold or failure. |
| **Recovery / rest days** | Stretch only | No lifting or core work — only when you take a rest day |

Tap **How hard should each set feel?** in Settings for the full breakdown.

### Browse program days

In Settings, use the program day carousel to preview upcoming days without changing today's workout.

### Rest (stretch) days

Need a break? While the rotating program is on, a **Rest day** button appears next to the program banner on the home screen. Tapping it:

- Creates a stretch day for today (the home screen shows the recovery stretches).
- Pushes the whole program back by one day, so today's planned workouts move to tomorrow.

Your weekly rest-day allowance depends on your training split, so training days plus rest days fill a real week (rolling 7-day window):

| Split | Rest days per week |
|---|---|
| 3-day | up to 4 |
| 4-day | up to 3 |
| 5-day | up to 2 |

When the limit is reached, the button disappears until a used rest day falls out of the window. Rest days are never auto-scheduled — only you decide when to take them.

## 11. Adjust settings

Open Settings from the gear icon on the home screen.

### Daily set target

Choose how many sets you aim to complete per exercise each day (when the rotating program is off). Options typically range from 1 to 5 sets.

### Rotating program

Toggle the plan on or off and pick your **training split** (3, 4, or 5 days). When on, set targets and exercise selection are managed by the program. Use the home-screen **Rest day** button when you want a stretch day — your split decides how many you get per week.

### Appearance

Switch between **day mode** and **night mode**.

### Language

Choose **English**, **עברית**, or **العربية**. The entire interface updates immediately, including right-to-left layout for Hebrew and Arabic.

### Weight units

Select **kg** or **lb** for logging and displaying load on weighted exercises. Plate increments adjust automatically (2.5 kg / 5 lb).

### FAQ

The FAQ section answers common training questions:

- Equipment needed to start
- Workout structure (push-pull-legs)
- Rep ranges for muscle growth
- When to progress to harder variations
- Weekly volume targets
- Nutrition and sleep basics

### Account

Tap **sign out** to end your session. Your progress remains saved and restores on next sign-in.

## 12. Track progress over time

### Coins

You earn coins after every completed set. Rewards are based on the exercise tier and the set duration, with a hard cap at 2 minutes:

> **Coins earned = min(max points for tier, ceil(min(duration in seconds, 120) / 120 × max points for tier))**

| Exercise tier | Max coins at 2 minutes |
|---|---:|
| Base | 10 |
| Pro | 20 |
| Elite | 30 |

Examples:

- A 1-minute Base exercise earns 5 coins.
- A 1-minute Pro exercise earns 10 coins.
- A 2-minute Elite exercise earns 30 coins.
- Any set longer than 2 minutes still earns the same maximum coins for that tier.

If a set goes over 2 minutes, the receipt recommends switching to a harder exercise in the Store or making the current move harder by adding weight. Spend coins in the store to unlock exercise variations. Your balance appears in the header on the home screen, store, and settings.

### Streaks

Complete at least two sets on a given day — they can be any exercises — to keep your streak alive. The flame badge on the home screen shows your current count. Missing a day resets the streak to zero.

If you miss a day, a **Restore streak** button appears on the home screen. Restore is always free, has no minimum streak length, and can bring the current streak back to your longest streak.

### Pushup daily goal

On push days, the pushups card shows progress toward a daily rep target (e.g., `45 / 100 pushups`) across all sets.

### Personal bests

Rep and weight personal bests are recorded automatically when you log sets. The summary screen highlights new records. Weighted exercises track your best **load × reps** combination.

## 13. Troubleshooting

### I cannot sign in

| Symptom | What to try |
|---|---|
| "Could not sign in" | Verify email and password. Use **Sign Up** if you have no account. |
| Google sign-in fails | Ensure pop-ups are allowed. Check that you selected the correct Google account. |
| Session does not restore | Sign out and sign in again. Check your internet connection. |

### Progress did not save

- Confirm you are signed in (progress is account-based).
- Complete the full flow: finish the set → log reps/weight → return home.
- If you see **Could not save this set**, check your connection and tap **log set** again.

### Camera / AI tracking issues

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **camera off** | Permission denied or no camera found | Grant camera access in browser settings and reload. |
| **camera paused** | Tracking paused manually | Tap **resume cam**. |
| **loading AI...** | Model is loading | Wait a few seconds. |
| **get in position** | Not in starting pose | Follow on-screen guidance for lighting, framing, and pose. |
| Tracking counts incorrectly | Pose not fully visible | Adjust angle, lighting, or distance. Use **timer only** as a fallback. |

### Store and lineup

| Issue | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Cannot afford an exercise | Complete more sets to earn coins. |
| Cannot equip upper exercise | You need 1 push + 1 pull equipped. Unequip one first if slots are full. |
| Equip buttons disabled | Turn off the rotating program in Settings to edit your lineup. |

### Data loading errors

If the home screen shows a red error banner:

- **Could not load set progress** → refresh the page.
- **Could not load workout stats** → check connection; streak may show a spinner until resolved.
- **Could not refresh profile** → sign out and back in.

## 14. Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **RIR (Reps in Reserve)** | How many more reps you could have done before failure. "1–2 RIR" means you stopped with 1–2 reps left in the tank. |
| **Set** | One continuous bout of an exercise, from start to finish. |
| **Lineup** | The exercises you have equipped and see on the home screen each day. |
| **Personal best (PR)** | Your highest recorded performance for an exercise — reps for bodyweight, load × reps for weighted moves. |
| **Rotating program** | The built-in RIR training cycle (3, 4, or 5-day split) that auto-selects exercises and set targets. Rest days are player-chosen. |
| **Progressive overload** | Gradually increasing training stress (more weight, reps, or difficulty) to keep getting stronger. |
| **Tier** | Exercise difficulty rating in the store: base, pro, or elite. |
